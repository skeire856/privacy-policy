Privacy Policy — Content Strategist App (CSA)

Content Strategist App (CSA) is a personal content-management tool, self-hosted and run locally by its owner. It is not a public service and does not have external users.

Data this app accesses

CSA uses the Facebook Graph API solely to publish content, created and approved by its owner, to a Facebook Page the owner administers. It requests only the minimum permissions needed for this: `pages_show_list`, `pages_read_engagement`, `pages_manage_posts`, and `business_management`.

Data storage and sharing

All content, credentials, and generated media are stored locally on the owner's own infrastructure. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for any purpose other than publishing the owner's own content to their own Facebook Page.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the app owner directly.
